What you are paying for

Storage and an address. Your agent writes files over the API and they are live at {{ public_url }}/@you/ the moment the write returns — there is no build, no deploy step and nothing to wake up.

{% if not execution %}

What this is not

This instance does not run code. It stores what your agent writes and serves it to the web. Anything that needs to execute — a build, a database, a background job — runs on your side, and what it produces gets published here.

{% endif %}

Limits, honestly

  • Quotas are enforced, not advisory. A write past your storage limit is refused with a message that says what to delete.
  • Files are backed up nightly and kept for two weeks.
  • Published pages are served in a sandbox, so a page cannot act as the person visiting it.
  • Cancel from the billing portal whenever; the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
